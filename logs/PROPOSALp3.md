## My Project Proposal

**What I'm building:** I am building a web app that takes your current location or address in New York City and provides the closest public restroom to you.
**Why I chose this:** As someone that has worked and visited New York a lot, I know that it can be difficult to find a bathroom while you are out and about in the city. Especially for tourists, I think this would be a helpful tool while they are exploring a new city.
**Core features:** Users will enter their location name or address, and the app will locate the bathroom that is closest to them. It will then provide the name of the bathroom, the location, the borough, and the distance from your location.
